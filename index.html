Who We Are

About South Chenab

South Chenab Seed Corporation (PVT) LTD. is a premier Seed Business Operator based in Rahim Yar Khan. Established with a vision to revolutionize Pakistan’s agriculture, we serve as a vital link between advanced breeding science and practical farming.

We are registered as a Basic Seed Producer, ensuring that the foundation of our country’s crops is strong, pure, and high-yielding.

Our Mission

To maximize farmer profitability by providing climate-resilient seeds, premium fertilizers, and hands-on technical guidance.
